Media & Speaking

Shaudé travels the world to speak, lead and connect. She brings a corporate executive’s rigor and a community advocate’s heart to every stage.

On stages around the world.

From global corporate summits to community town halls, Shaudé has spoken on international stages and led teams that span continents. As Global Corporate President of Amazon’s Black Employee Network (BEN) she connected members and leaders across the world, and as a community leader at home she speaks to marchers, students, executives and policymakers alike.

Audiences remember her because she tells the whole story: where she started, what it took, and what she learned along the way. She speaks with warmth and directness, and she leaves rooms with a plan.
