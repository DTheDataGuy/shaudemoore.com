Awards & Recognition

Honored by business, civic and community institutions for showing up, building up and lifting others along the way.

2024

Puget Sound Business Journal

40 Under 40

Each year the Puget Sound Business Journal names forty leaders under the age of 40 who are shaping the region through their work and their community ties. Shaudé was named to the 2024 class, chosen by a panel of judges from a large field of applicants and celebrated alongside the region’s most influential young leaders.

Puget Sound Business Journal

2025

Exceptional organizational leadership in community development

George Washington Trailblazer Award

The George Washington Trailblazer Award honors Shaudé’s exceptional organizational leadership in community development: building institutions, partnerships and plans that put opportunity and ownership back in community hands.

2019

Martin Luther King Jr. Commemoration

Community Organizing Award

Presented in April 2019, the Martin Luther King Jr. Commemoration Community Organizing Award recognized her grassroots organizing to keep Dr. King’s call alive in Seattle and King County, work she has carried forward as chair of the Seattle MLK Jr. Organizing Coalition.
