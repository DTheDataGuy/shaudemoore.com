The MLK legacy

“Dr. King had challenged the community, the world, really, to think more about the building of community and building on the foundation of justice, democracy and love within the chaos itself.”

Shaudé Moore, in the Bellingham Herald, January 2026

Youngest woman. First woman.

Shaudé made history as the youngest woman voted chair of the Seattle Martin Luther King Jr. Organizing Coalition and the first woman to lead it, carrying forward a grassroots tradition that fills Garfield High School and the streets of King County every January.

Where do we go from here?

Under her leadership the coalition pairs the march and rally with a Youth Impact Forum and the MLK Opportunity Fair, connecting King’s call to jobs, training and young leaders.

44th

The 2027 march will be the 44th in King County.

“When you march, when you think of voting, when you volunteer and you’re raising your voice for justice, are you thinking about answering Dr. King’s call?”

The McKinney Center.

A Civil Rights era landmark, back in community hands.

The six-story building at 2120 S Jackson Street opened in 1974 as the Seattle Opportunities Industrialization Center, Rev. Dr. Samuel B. McKinney’s vision for job training and Black economic mobility. As the first CEO of the Central District Community Preservation and Development Authority, Shaudé leads its restoration as a home for Black entrepreneurship, education, culture and ownership.

The chronology

Scroll to move through the years

  1. 2020

    A founding seat at the table

    Serves as vice president and secretary of the CDCPDA board from its inception.

  2. 2021

    The building comes home

    Ownership of the former Seattle Vocational Institute transfers to the CDCPDA, returning the property to the community it was built to serve.

  3. 2023Aug

    First CEO

    Named the first chief executive of the McKinney Center, charged with leading a roughly $30 million renovation vision.

  4. 2023Sep

    $1.47M for renewal

    The City of Seattle’s Equitable Development Initiative awards $1,473,204 toward rehabilitation of the center.

  5. 2025May

    Black Homeownership Hub

    Launches a partnership with Black Legacy Homeowners to advance homeownership and build generational wealth.

  6. 2025Oct

    Building decarbonization

    Secures an Office of Sustainability & Environment grant for energy efficiency in the historic building.

  7. 2026Apr

    A centennial celebration

    Hosts Springing Forward, honoring Rev. McKinney’s 100th birthday.

  8. 2026Sep

    A Seattle Landmark

    The Landmarks Preservation Board votes 8–0 to designate the McKinney Center, protecting its site, exterior, atrium stair and atrium volume.

  9. 2026Oct

    CD Connected

    Co-hosts a community conversation on AI and digital equity inside the center.
